ONDRIK(1)

NAME
    ondrik - seals of texts and knots of numbers

SYNOPSIS
    ondrik press [--turns N] TEXT
    ondrik tie [--give] A B

DESCRIPTION
    ondrik prints the seal of a text, or the knot of two whole numbers,
    on one line.

COMMANDS
    press [--turns N] TEXT
        Print the seal of TEXT: 10 signs, digits and lowercase letters.
        Give TEXT as one argument; quote it when it holds spaces. TEXT
        does not start with -, which starts an option.

        --turns N
            Fold the seal N times (N is from 1 to 1000000). Without
            it the seal is folded once. "The seal of TEXT folded 3
            times" is `ondrik press --turns 3 TEXT`.

    tie [--give] A B
        Print the knot of the whole numbers A and B: 6 digits, zeros
        at the start kept. The knot of A and B is not the knot of B
        and A.

        --give
            Tie the slack knot instead. "The slack knot of 12 and 40"
            is `ondrik tie --give 12 40`.

    The options come right after the command, before its other
    arguments.

EXIT STATUS
    0 when it printed its result; 2 when it cannot read its arguments,
    with a message on standard error.

EXAMPLES
    ondrik press hello
    ondrik press --turns 4 "a b c"
    ondrik tie 7 99
    ondrik tie --give 7 99
