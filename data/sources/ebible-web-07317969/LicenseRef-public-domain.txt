The World English Bible's copyright page (https://ebible.org/engwebp/copyright.htm) says:
"The World English Bible is in the Public Domain. That means that it is not copyrighted."
Its only condition concerns the name: if the text is changed, the result is not to be called the
World English Bible. "World English Bible" is a trademark of eBible.org.

eBible.org publishes it from the United States. The page states no restriction on AI or machine
learning use.
