Plato died in 347 BC. Benjamin Jowett (1817-1893) translated the Dialogues, first published in
1871 by the Clarendon Press, Oxford, United Kingdom; this text follows his own revised third
edition of 1892.

United States: published before 1931. United Kingdom (country of origin): every author and
translator died more than 70 years ago.
