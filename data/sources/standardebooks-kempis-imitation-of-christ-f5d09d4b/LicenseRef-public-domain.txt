Thomas à Kempis died in 1471. William Benham (1831-1910) translated it, first published in 1874;
this text follows the Tauchnitz edition of 1877. The notes are by H. C. Beeching (1859-1919).

United States: published before 1931. Every other country: every author, translator and annotator
died more than 80 years ago.
