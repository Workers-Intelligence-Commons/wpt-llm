Epictetus died around 135, Arrian in the 2nd century. George Long (1800-1879) translated the
Discourses, first published in 1877 by George Bell, London, United Kingdom (Bohn's Classical
Library).

United States: published before 1931. United Kingdom (country of origin): every author and
translator died more than 70 years ago.
