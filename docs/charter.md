# Charter (draft)

A calm tone and plain rules. Nothing here is final.

## Statement of intent

Workers' Intelligence Commons builds open artificial intelligence tools, so
that intelligence stays a shared means of production: everyone's, or rather
no one's. Its first model, WPT (Workers' Pre-trained Transformer), is a
language model trained from scratch on content whose license is clear, and
published under copyleft.

## Charter

These rules apply to Workers' Intelligence Commons, to each of its models
(starting with WPT), and to everyone who contributes.

1. **A common good.** Intelligence is a means of production. It belongs to
   everyone, or rather to no one, and must stay accessible to workers: the
   proletariat of yesterday and today, and future generations.
2. **Copyleft.** The project is published under copyleft
   (AGPL-3.0-or-later). Every improvement served to the public stays open.
3. **Clear licenses.** Only content whose license is clear (MIT, public
   domain, etc.) enters the corpus. When in doubt, we refuse.
4. **Provenance.** Every admitted content is documented: a pointer to the
   source, a checksum, its license and the human decision that admitted it.
   The record is public.
5. **Training from scratch.** No distillation from another company's model,
   and no existing base model to fine-tune.
6. **Service and self-hosting.** The service can be paid for (reliability,
   support, usage limits); the intelligence cannot. Self-hosting always
   stays possible.
7. **Competition.** It is welcome. Whoever improves WPT and serves it to the
   public must give the improvements back to the commons.
8. **Transparency.** We publish the recipes, and a release of the models
   each month that brought enough progress, so that others can take over
   at their own scale ([publishing.md](publishing.md)).
